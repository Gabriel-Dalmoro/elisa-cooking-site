Universal Billing, Invoicing & Tax Credit Architecture

The Real Revenue Weapon

"Neutral Core Architecture, Country-Specific Tax Adapters"

In-home batch cooking across Europe shares one universal truth: the service happens in the client's home, only labour is eligible for home-service tax reductions, and materials/groceries must be excluded. ChefOS builds this universal primitive into its core data model, and layers thin regional adapters on top.

Auto-Split Labour vs Débours (Food)

A chef charging "€200 session, groceries included" burns their micro-enterprise VAT threshold (€37,500 ceiling) on groceries. Because ChefOS knows the grocery list, it automatically splits the invoice: Labour (€140 creditable) + Food Débours (€60 reimbursed at cost with receipts). This extends how long a chef stays in the simple tax regime.

E-Invoicing Mandate (Factur-X 2026/2027)

From September 2026 in France, every micro-enterprise must receive electronic invoices via approved platforms; issuing becomes mandatory in 2027. Thousands of French chefs must change their invoicing. ChefOS partners with an approved platform to offer native Factur-X compliance without building heavy tax rails.

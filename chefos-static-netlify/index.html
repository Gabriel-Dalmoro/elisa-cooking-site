Current Production Implementation

The core mechanics are already built, tested, and actively powering daily operations in Elisa's live production environment:

/admin/semaine LIVE PRODUCTION
Weekly Operations Hub & Calendar Sync

Google Calendar integration with slot detection (Matin/Après-midi), client matching, selection status badges, and 1-click customized WhatsApp magic link generator.

/admin/recettes LIVE PRODUCTION
Recipe Vault & Menu Builder

Persistent recipe library categorized by tags (Meat, Fish, Veg, Vegan) with historical usage tracking (times_used, last_used_week) and 1-click menu compilation.

/admin/clients LIVE PRODUCTION
Client CRM & Household Dietary Shield

Persistent client memory: portion sizing (2 vs 4 people), household allergies, kitchen equipment (induction/gas), door codes, and unique token generation.

/admin/cuisine/[clientId] LIVE PRODUCTION
Interactive Kitchen Mode with Wake Lock

Optimized for greasy fingers on tablets. Native Web Screen Wake Lock prevents tablet dimming, with integrated cooking timers and high-visibility allergy alerts.

/choisir?token=... LIVE PRODUCTION
Client Selection Magic Portal

Zero-password client interface. Clients tap to pick their 4 dishes, add special requests, and submit in 30 seconds with immediate sync to the chef's dashboard.

/admin/menu-visuel LIVE PRODUCTION
1-Click Instagram Story Studio

Instantly renders the current weekly menu into an on-brand 9:16 Instagram Story flyer, eliminating hours spent manually designing on Canva.

04

Core Product Architecture & Pillars

1. Smart Grocery Aggregator

Auto-multiplies ingredients by household portions and sums totals across all weekly orders into a categorized in-store shopping checklist (Fishmonger, Butcher, Produce, Dry Goods).

Saves 2 hrs every Monday morning

2. Multi-Chef Brigade Mode

Assign sessions to assistant chefs. Assistants receive a scoped mobile login viewing only their assigned cooking sheet, entry codes, and allergies—with zero access to billing or financials.

Enables scaling past 30 hrs/wk

3. Dietary Allergy Shield

Automated conflict detection. High-visibility warnings prevent clients from choosing allergen-conflicting dishes, eliminating chef liability in high-end households.

Zero liability & peace of mind
